A BIT ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Anthony

I'm a product designer from Roseville, California, studying design at UC Davis. I got here the roundabout way: a high-school graphic design class, then two years of computer science, before I finally admitted the thing I actually loved was design.

Being my family's unofficial tech support taught me early how much a badly designed product costs people. That is the whole reason I do UX. Good, quiet, intuitive design makes someone's day a little easier, and that is worth getting right.

When I'm not in Figma you'll catch me making beats, out on a run, or losing a ranked Tekken match.
