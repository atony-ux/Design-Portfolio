Prod. Davis

Co-Brand Design Director · UC Davis music production club

Prod. Davis is UC Davis's music production club, open to everyone from first-time beatmakers to people already putting out music. Every Thursday there's an A-side meeting built around a challenge, and on Tuesdays a B-side session for workshops, office hours and hanging out.

As Co-Brand Design Director, I work on the club's look and its weekly event graphics. The series shares one system so every post reads as Prod. Davis at a glance: the folder-path header, halftone cutouts on torn paper, a taped note for the B-side, and the same bold blue titles.
