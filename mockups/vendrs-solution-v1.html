THE PROBLEM

Many small businesses want to scale, but lack the resources, staff, and opportunity to do so

Running a small business means juggling operations, finances, and growth all at once. Often times small business owners struggle to discover local selling opportunities, connect with other business owners nearby, and find the tools and resources they need to run their operations more efficiently.

Operations, spread across spreadsheets and group chats

Discovery, usually after the spot was already gone

Community, one DM at a time, nothing that stuck

This often meant the difference between a table at Saturday's market and finding out about it Sunday morning.

How might we help small business owners operate their businesses, share resources, and connect with their local community through an accessible and intuitive platform?

The question we designed against
SOLUTION

Vendrs brings discovery, resources, and operations into one platform

We delivered a high-fidelity prototype built exclusively for small business owners, around three core pages. A Community feed surfaces nearby vending opportunities before they fill up, a Marketplace puts templates and operational guides within reach, and a Dashboard keeps finances and performance visible without a spreadsheet.

Community

A feed for discovering and applying to local vending opportunities.

Marketplace

Business resources in one place: Excel templates, operational guides, and the tools owners need to run things.

Dashboard

Finances and business performance, tracked in one view.

RESEARCH

They didn't lack ambition. They lacked infrastructure.

20+ survey responses showed how common each problem was, and 10 interviews with Gen Z vendors selling art, food and services at UC Davis markets showed why it mattered. Three numbers set the priorities.

97%want a bigger audience for what they make
80%run the entire shop by themselves
70%want to scale, with no system to do it
