Studio filter options

Click around: every column works. All three hand-drawn versions use drawably, the sketch library already on the site (it draws the "More about me" button and the tags), so nothing new gets added. Strokes are drawn once and stay still, so they cost nothing while videos play.

Today

Straight lines and square checkboxes.

A. Sketch checkboxes

Hand-drawn boxes, a pen tick that draws in (and overshoots the box), and pencil lines between rows.

B. Circled in ink

No boxes. Picking a filter circles the word in blue pen, like marking a list on paper. Counts are handwritten.

C. Marker swipe

No boxes. A picked filter gets a light blue highlighter swipe and a sketched tick.

Other libraries I looked at

  • RoughNotation: animated circles, underlines, highlights and brackets drawn over text. Close to options B and C, but it would be a second sketch library on the site.
  • Rough.js: the drawing engine this style comes from. Lower level: every shape would be built by hand.
  • Wired Elements: ready-made hand-drawn web components (checkbox, radio, toggle). Heavier, and the boxes have a thicker cartoon look than the rest of the site.
  • PaperCSS / Doodle CSS: whole hand-drawn CSS themes. They would fight the site's own styles rather than fit into one section.
